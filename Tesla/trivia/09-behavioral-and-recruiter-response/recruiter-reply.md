# Recruiter reply — template

You did not paste the recruiter's actual bullet points, so this covers the ones that email
almost always contains. **Delete what does not apply, and fill in the bracketed parts — do
not send anything you have not verified about yourself.**

The tone to aim for: short, direct, answers in the same order they asked, no filler. A
recruiter is triaging dozens of these; being easy to process is the whole game.

## Notes on the individual answers

**Term availability.** If you can genuinely do either, say so — it doubles your chances of
matching an opening. If you have a real constraint, state it plainly rather than hedging;
"either, preferring Summer" is fine, "possibly, depending" is not.

**Work authorization.** Answer it directly. Recruiters have to ask and vagueness here gets
applications parked. If you need sponsorship, say which and when.

**Relevant experience.** Resist listing everything. Two or three sentences, chosen for
*this* posting: C++, real-time or embedded, hardware in the loop, performance measurement,
test infrastructure. The humanoid arm work is your strongest card because it is real
hardware with real consequences — lead with it.

**C++ honesty.** If your C++ is newer than your other skills, say what you are doing about
it rather than overclaiming. An interviewer will find out in twenty minutes either way, and
"here is my deliberate practice, with artifacts" is a much better position than a claim that
collapses.

**Length.** The whole reply should fit on one screen. If it does not, cut the experience
paragraph, not the logistics.

**What not to include:** salary expectations (unless they asked), a long explanation of why
you are excited, apologies for anything, or a restatement of your resume.

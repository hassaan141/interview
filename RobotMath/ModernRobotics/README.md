## How to study each lesson

Use one 45–60 minute session per row:

1. Watch the video once without pausing.
2. Watch it again and write definitions and one example in
   [`chapter_02_configuration_space/notes.md`](chapter_02_configuration_space/notes.md).
3. Do the matching prompts in the notes from memory.
4. Run `venv/bin/python RobotMath/ModernRobotics/chapter_02_configuration_space/practice.py`.
5. Explain the idea aloud in two minutes, without notation.

## Math to know first

For Chapter 2, you mainly need algebra, basic trigonometry, and the idea that a
vector is an ordered list of coordinates. Do not delay Chapter 2 to study all
of linear algebra first.

Before Chapter 3, review:

- vectors, dot products, cross products, and norms;
- matrix multiplication, transpose, inverse, and identity matrices;
- radians, sine, cosine, and the unit circle;
- derivatives as rates of change.

## What comes next

After Chapter 2, create a Chapter 3 folder for rotation matrices, angular
velocity, exponential coordinates, homogeneous transforms, twists, and
wrenches. That chapter is the core robot-motion math, and Chapter 2 gives its
symbols physical meaning first.
